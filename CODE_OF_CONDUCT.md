# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarized here. The Covenant is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep the discussion about the project and the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code. Report a problem privately by e-mail to **[bang9ming9@gmail.com](mailto:bang9ming9@gmail.com)**; do not post it publicly.

Maintainers will treat reports confidentially and respect the privacy of the person who reported.
